  tokens, context 8192, greedy seed0, ignore-EOS, prefix caching and speculation off.
- BF16 decoder and configured FP32 head; the observed vendor head weights remain BF16.
- Each configuration has 10 excluded warmups, 30 measured requests and two
  untimed diagnostics. Cohorts ran sequentially, not cross-engine ABBA.
- Both Ferric configurations match all 42 internal output-token sequences and
  UTF-8 responses, 5,376 output token IDs each. Vendor warmup/timed streams
  match exact UTF-8 and usage; token IDs are available only in its two untimed
  diagnostics, not in its timed streaming responses.
- Each included cohort has clean, unsignaled owned teardown. Every archive
  member, plan, CPU qualification, raw response, endpoint sample and metric
  calculation is independently checked by a data-only audit.
- TTFT and TPOT come from HTTP SSE text arrival, not true token interarrival or
  shader durations. Finite output rate includes request drain and is not
  sustained-load throughput. Clock, power and temperature equivalence are not
  established; root-visible descriptor observations are finite.
